## About us

3WAVES is a web agency that builds fast, search-optimised websites, online stores and custom web applications. Our team has worked with businesses from many industries for over 15 years. We combine clean, hand-written code with a clear focus on what matters to our clients: visibility on Google, more enquiries and a website that works in everyday business.

We work in **Polish, German and English** and support clients in Frankfurt am Main, across Germany and in Poland.

## What we do

| Service | What it includes |

| **Website development** | Individually coded company websites, landing pages and one-pagers, WordPress and WooCommerce stores |
| **SEO** | Technical SEO, Core Web Vitals, content optimisation, local SEO |
